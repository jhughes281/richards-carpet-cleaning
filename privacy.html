Privacy policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

What we collect

When you send a quote request we collect your name, phone number, email (if you give one), the job address and ZIP code, the service and date you picked, your estimate selections, any notes you write, and the date and wording of the contact consent you agreed to.

To stop spam we also keep a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address. It can't be turned back into your IP address. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use it

  • To call, text or email you about your quote and appointment.
  • To keep a record of the price we estimated, so it doesn't change on you later.
  • To block repeated or automated form submissions.

We don't sell your information or share it for marketing.

Text messages

If you tick the consent box, Richard may text you about your quote and appointment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or START to opt back in. Consent is not a condition of buying anything.

Who handles it for us

Your request is stored with Supabase (database hosting). Texts are sent through Twilio and emails through Resend. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the form was filled in by a person. Each of these companies processes data only to provide that service.

How long we keep it

We keep quote and job records for as long as we need them to serve you and for our business and tax records. Ask us and we'll delete your information unless we're required to keep it.
