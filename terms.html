Terms & guarantee

Last updated October 3, 2026

Estimates

Online estimates are based on what you select: number of rooms and add-ons. Prices are before tax. Richard confirms the final price on site, before any work starts. If the job is different from what was selected, for example more rooms or heavy staining, he'll tell you the new price first, and you can decline.

Tile & grout and commercial jobs are priced on site.

Online discount

When a promo code is shown on the site, it applies to quote requests sent through the online form. One code per job.

Re-clean guarantee

If you're not happy with a cleaned area, tell us within 14 days (30 days on the Platinum package) and we'll re-clean that area free.

If we can't remove a stain, we tell you before we start, and there's no charge for that area.

Access

The truck-mount unit runs from the truck, so we need driveway or curbside access near your door. You don't need to provide water or power. Please clear breakables and electronics before we arrive; we move the furniture.
